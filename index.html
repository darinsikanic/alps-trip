JFK Airport · Delta Terminal 4

Arrival  ·  Day 2
2
Arrive MXP → Courmayeur
Sunday, March 29
Drive Gear rental Cadran Solaire
▾
11:49 AM — Arrive
Milan Malpensa Airport (MXP)
Clear customs and immigration. Head to Budget Car Rental in the terminal to pick up your vehicle.
~12:30 PM — Drive
MXP → Courmayeur  ·  ~1h 45min
Take the A26 → A5 motorway toward Aosta and Courmayeur. Spectacular mountain scenery as you approach. You'll pass through the Mont Blanc tunnel area.
~2:30 PM — Check-in
Le Géant, Courmayeur — Apartments by Marriott Bonvoy
Drop bags and get settled. Standard check-in time 3:00 PM — call ahead if you want to request early access to your room.
Afternoon
Ski Gear Rental
Rent skis, boots, poles, and — critically — full avalanche safety equipment (beacon/transceiver, probe, shovel) for Tuesday's Vallée Blanche. Several rental shops are in town and at the Courmayeur ski area base. Confirm your beacon is working and that everyone in the group knows how to use it.
~5:00 PM
Rest & freshen up
You've been traveling for ~18+ hours. Take a nap, shower, and get ready for dinner. You've earned it.
7:30 PM — Dinner (Reserved)
Ristorante Cadran Solaire
Reservation confirmed at 7:30 PM. Classic Valle d'Aosta cuisine — a perfect welcome dinner after the journey.
Ski Day 1  ·  Day 3
3
Ski Courmayeur Mont Blanc
Monday, March 30
Full ski day La Chaumière / Chiecco Après
▾
Courmayeur Mont Blanc — lifts typically open 8:30 AM. First tracks on a Monday = fewer crowds.
Early Morning
Breakfast & kit up
Grab a quick espresso and cornetto at a local bar. Get your avalanche gear fitted and tested — even if you're not doing the VB until Tuesday, it's good to get comfortable with the beacon today.
8:30 AM — Lifts open
First runs on Courmayeur
Take the Courmayeur gondola or Dolonne cable car up to Plan Checrouit. Explore the groomed runs in the morning while the snow is best. The resort covers 100km of pistes with incredible Mont Blanc views.
~12:00 PM — Mountain Lunch
La Chaumière or Chiecco
Both are popular on-mountain restaurants at Plan Checrouit. La Chaumière is beloved for its sun terrace and hearty Valle d'Aosta dishes; Chiecco is lively and great for a long sunny lunch. Aim to arrive by noon before the rush — or book ahead if possible.
Afternoon
Continue skiing
Explore different sectors — the Val Veny side offers beautiful off-piste options if you're feeling adventurous (warm-up for Tuesday). Lifts typically close 4:30–5:00 PM.
~5:00 PM — Après
Après Ski  Bar Roma Le Privé
Bar Roma — Stone-walled classic right in the village center. Comes alive around 5 PM with a complimentary canapés spread. Order a Bombardino (the Italian ski après classic: egg liqueur + brandy + whipped cream).

Le Privé — Superb handcrafted cocktails and delicious tapas. Warm, friendly atmosphere and a bit more sophisticated.
~7:30 PM — Dinner
Simple dinner  du Tunnel La Terrazza
Pizzeria Ristorante du Tunnel — Oldest pizzeria in Courmayeur, wood-oven pies and classic mountain dishes. Chalet-style with an al fresco terrace. Very popular — book ahead.

Ristorante La Terrazza — Central village location, excellent regional Italian cuisine in a homey, relaxed setting. Perfect for tired skiers.
Vallée Blanche  ·  Day 4
4
Vallée Blanche — Off-Piste Descent
Tuesday, March 31
Guided descent Chamonix Pierre Alexis 1877
▾
Avalanche Safety: Bring your full ABCs — Avalanche beacon, Probe, Shovel. Your guide will brief you on safety protocols. Listen carefully.
Booking via explore-share.com — confirm guide meeting point and time in advance.
Very Early Morning
Meet your guide
Guides typically start early — confirm the exact meeting time and location. Dress warmly in layers; the Vallée Blanche starts at ~3,800m and can be bitterly cold at the top even in spring.
Morning
Aiguille du Midi — Top of the descent
Take the Aiguille du Midi cable car to 3,842m. The famous arête (ridge walk) with crampons gets you to the start of the descent. Breathtaking views of the Mont Blanc massif.
Morning → Midday
The Vallée Blanche descent (~24km)
The classic route descends through the glacial Vallée Blanche, weaving between crevasse fields and seracs with your guide. Allow 3–5 hours for the full descent depending on conditions and group pace. The scenery is unlike anything else in the Alps.
Midday / Early Afternoon
Arrive in Chamonix
The descent ends at the Mer de Glace station in Chamonix. Grab lunch in town — you'll have earned it. Chamonix has great mountain restaurants and cafés along the main street.
Afternoon
Uber back to Courmayeur
~30–40 minutes via the Mont Blanc Tunnel (toll). Relax and let the day sink in. Return to the hotel, shower, and rest before dinner.
Evening — Dinner (Reserved)
Restaurant Pierre Alexis 1877
Reservation confirmed. One of Courmayeur's most celebrated restaurants, set in a historic 19th-century building. A fitting celebration dinner after a legendary day on the mountain.
Ski Day 2  ·  Day 5
5
Ski Courmayeur Mont Blanc
Wednesday, April 1
Full ski day Alpetta / Maison Vieille Après
▾
Morning
Full day on the mountain
You know the mountain now — explore different runs and sectors. The Val Veny area offers great varied terrain. Explore above Plan Checrouit for Mont Blanc panoramas.
~12:00 PM — Mountain Lunch
Mix it up  Alpetta Maison Vieille
Alpetta Mountain Restaurant — Wholesome, locally sourced cooking with friendly English-speaking service. Gourmet burgers, mountain fare. A low-key gem between Chiecco and Chateau Branlant. Call +39 349 3991363.

Maison Vieille (1,956m) — One of the most spectacular lunch spots in the Alps, on a sunny plateau above Plan Checrouit via a slow double chair. Exceptional food and wine. Book ahead — very popular.
Afternoon
Last ski runs in Courmayeur
Make the most of the final day here — soak in the Mont Blanc views. Pack up skis by 4:30 PM so you have time to prepare for the drive tomorrow.
~5:00 PM — Après
Après Ski  Bar Roma Le Privé
Return to Bar Roma for another Bombardino, or try Le Privé for a last handcrafted cocktail. Also worth trying: Le Dahu, which has an outdoor courtyard with sheepskin blankets — cozy on a spring evening.
~7:30 PM — Dinner
Simple dinner  du Tunnel La Terrazza
Return to a favourite from Monday, or explore a new spot. Valle d'Aosta specialties to look for: fonduta (fondue), polenta taragna, and local Fontina cheese dishes. Early dinner is smart — you have a drive tomorrow morning.
Tonight: Pack up your gear bags and double-check ski equipment to be returned in the morning.

Zermatt

April 2 – 4  ·  3 nights  ·  Matterhorn country

Wallis · Valais
Travel Day  ·  Day 6
6
Courmayeur → Zermatt
Thursday, April 2
Drive to Täsch Train to Zermatt Gear hire
▾
Early Morning
Return ski gear & check out
Return rental skis and avalanche equipment before checking out of Le Géant. Load up the car and aim to be on the road by 8–9 AM.
~9:00 AM — Drive
Courmayeur → Matterhorn Terminal Täsch  ·  ~2h 45min
Route: A5 → Great St. Bernard Tunnel (toll) → Martigny → Sion → Visp → Täsch. Beautiful drive through the Swiss Rhône Valley with snow-capped peaks all around. Park at Matterhorn Terminal Täsch (paid car park — book ahead at matterhornparadise.ch or pay on arrival).
~12:00 PM
Shuttle train: Täsch → Zermatt  ·  12 minutes
Zermatt is car-free — the shuttle train runs every 20 minutes. Trains run from ~6 AM to midnight. Fare is CHF ~10 each way (buy at station or via SBB app).
~12:30 PM — Check-in
Hotel Alpen Resort & SPA
A well-appointed hotel with multiple restaurants on site — Peakfine (creative modern cuisine) and Ribis & Stibis (cosy Alpine fondue and mountain fare). Great for at least one dinner in house. Breakfast buffet 7–11 AM daily.
Afternoon
Ski gear hire  Bayard Sport Matterhorn Sport
Bayard Sport — Multiple locations including right next to the Sunnegga Express lift. Well-established, excellent range. Book online for a discount at bayardsport.ch.

Matterhorn Sport — On the main road between the train station and Furi gondola, and a central village location. Good value and convenient.
~5:00 PM — Après
First Zermatt après  Hennu Stall The Broken Bar
Hennu Stall — Family-run charmer in the Furi hamlet at the base of the Furi-Zermatt slope. Often called the best après in Zermatt. Authentic, dynamic, traditional wood-and-slate chalet vibes.

The Broken Bar (Hotel Post) — A legendary cellar bar with 50+ years of après history. Iconic Zermatt institution.
Evening — Dinner
Hotel Alpen Resort & SPA restaurants
Tonight's a good night to stay in and try the hotel. Ribis & Stibis offers Alpine cosiness with traditional fondues and gourmet burgers — perfect after a travel day. Or Peakfine for creative cuisine if you're feeling more upscale.
Ski Day 3  ·  Day 7
7
Ski Zermatt Matterhorn
Friday, April 3
Full ski day @paradise 12pm Elsie's
▾
Zermatt Matterhorn — 360km of pistes across 3 countries (Switzerland, Italy, France). Consider a Cervinia day if conditions are good.
Early Morning
First cable car — get the best snow
Head to Matterhorn Glacier Paradise (Klein Matterhorn, 3,883m) for the highest terrain. Morning light on the Matterhorn from up here is extraordinary. Lifts open ~8:30 AM.
12:00 PM — Lunch (Reserved)
@paradise Restaurant — 3,883m
The highest restaurant in the Alps, at the top of the Matterhorn Glacier Paradise cable car. Reservation at 12:00 PM. Confirm directly with the restaurant. The views are simply unmatched — you're standing above almost everything.
Afternoon
Explore the Matterhorn ski area
Work your way down through Trockener Steg and the Furi area. The Sunnegga sector offers great tree skiing and some of the most iconic Matterhorn views on the mountain.
~5:00 PM — Après / Wind-down
Elsie's Wine & Champagne Bar  Elsie's Bar
Elsie's Bar on Kirchplatz — a Zermatt institution. Legendary wine and champagne selection, first-class food (oysters, Swiss delicacies), golden-twenties ambience. Refined and intimate — perfect for a Friday evening wind-down.
Evening — Dinner
North Wall Bar & Restaurant
A Zermatt favourite for its relaxed, convivial atmosphere. Good food, cold beer, great vibes — exactly what Friday night calls for.
Ben's 50th  ·  Day 8
8
Ski + Ben's 50th Birthday
Saturday, April 4
Morning ski Chez Vrony 11:30am MX Zermatt
▾
Morning
Morning ski runs
Get out early and make the most of the last ski morning. Head to Sunnegga sector — easy access for getting back down to Chez Vrony for lunch. Aim to finish your last run by 11:15 AM.
11:30 AM — Lunch (Reserved)
Chez Vrony
One of the most beloved mountain restaurants in Zermatt, perched on the Sunnegga slopes with jaw-dropping Matterhorn views. Known for exceptional Swiss cuisine and a warm, rustic atmosphere. The perfect send-off lunch. Reservation at 11:30 AM.
Afternoon
Last ski runs of the trip
After lunch, take a few final runs before skiing down and returning your hire gear. Then head back to the hotel to get ready for the evening.
~4:00 PM
Return ski gear & get ready
Return hire equipment. Take full advantage of the hotel spa — you've earned a long soak. Get dressed up for the evening.
Evening — Ben's 50th Birthday
Heinz Julen Loft — MX Zermatt
One of Zermatt's most extraordinary private spaces — a spectacular architect-designed loft by the legendary Heinz Julen, with incredible Matterhorn views. A night to remember.

Milan

April 5 – 6  ·  Drive in, fly out

Lombardia
Travel Day  ·  Day 9
9
Zermatt → Milan
Sunday, April 5
Last runs? ~3h 30min drive Milan evening
▾
Morning
Sleep in & optional ski runs
After Ben's 50th, take it easy this morning. If you're feeling fresh, squeeze in a final few runs before 10 AM — then ski gear back and check out. Or enjoy breakfast at the hotel and soak up the last Matterhorn views.
~10:30–11:00 AM
Train: Zermatt → Täsch  ·  12 min
Collect your car from Matterhorn Terminal Täsch. Load up and head toward Milan.
~11:30 AM — Drive
Täsch → Milan  ·  ~3h 30min
Route via Visp → Simplon Pass (if open) or via Brig → Domodossola → A26 → Milan. The Simplon route is spectacular through the Swiss-Italian Alps. Arrive Milan early afternoon.
~3:00 PM — Check-in
Hotel Indigo Milan – Corso Monforte (IHG)
Boutique IHG hotel in the upscale Porta Venezia/Monforte district — great for exploring the Brera and Porta Nuova areas on foot. Drop the car or find nearby parking — Milan city driving can be tricky.
Afternoon
Explore Milan
The hotel is well-placed for a stroll. Brera district (30 min walk) is charming with galleries, boutiques and aperitivo bars. Or take the metro to the Duomo area for a quick look at Milan's iconic cathedral and the stunning Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II.
Evening — Dinner (TBD)
A nice dinner in Milan  Trattoria del Nuovo Macello Navigli area
Trattoria del Nuovo Macello — Classic Milanese trattoria, beloved by locals. Old-school risotto alla Milanese and ossobuco. Book ahead.

Navigli district — The canal area (~20 min by taxi) has dozens of great restaurants for a relaxed final evening, from pizza to modern Italian. Great atmosphere on a Sunday night.

Monday, April 6
Breakfast Sights DL 185 at 2:15pm
▾
Flight DL 185 departs MXP at 2:15 PM. Aim to be at the airport by 12:00 PM (noon). Return the rental car before check-in.
Morning
Breakfast somewhere special  Pasticceria Marchesi
Pasticceria Marchesi (Via Monte Napoleone) — Milan's most elegant historic pastry shop, dating to 1824. Impeccable croissants, pastries, and coffee. A perfect final Italian morning.
~9:30 AM
Quick Milan sights
If time allows: Piazza del Duomo and Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II are 5 minutes apart and always worth a look. The hotel is well-placed for a quick loop before heading to the airport.

Drive to MXP  ·  ~45min from central Milan
Return rental car at MXP Budget location. Allow extra time for car return and check-in. MXP terminal is straightforward once you're in.
